KAHRAMAN – Time tracking that asks
==================================

Kahraman lives as a small clock face in the notification area of the taskbar. At an
interval of your choice (1 minute to 24 hours) it asks what you are working on.
Your answers add up to a report: where did how much time go?

How time is counted
-------------------
• Each entry stands for the time since the previous entry.
• The first entry of a day only marks the start of work and does not count itself.
  Tip: just log "Start" in the morning.
• A new day begins at midnight. If you finish after midnight, log "Start" again
  first thing the next morning.
• Hours are shown as decimals (1 h 15 min = 1.25); a day has 8 hours.

Logging
-------
• The activity (left) is required; sub-activity (middle) and comment (right) are optional.
• The narrow strip on the far right (or Alt+K) collapses and expands the comment field; when collapsed, the lists get wider.
• The narrow strip to the right of the sub-activities (or Alt+U) collapses and expands them. When collapsed, they only appear if a sub-activity is suggested for the activity; Kahraman remembers whether you last expanded or collapsed them.
  Normally it is collapsed and expands by itself when there is a comment for the selected project
  (the one used most recently is suggested). To turn this off: Settings > General.
• The most recently used entries are shown in bold at the top of the lists.
• F3 in the activity field jumps to the next activity that contains the typed text.
  Up/down arrows scroll through the list.
• For a new activity, Kahraman asks whether it should be remembered for later.
• Right-click in the lists: highlight entries in color or remove them from the list.
  Time already logged stays in the report.
• Right-click in the sub-activity list: insert or edit templates.
• The question stays on top of all other windows until it is answered.
  As long as you have never clicked it, Kahraman tries to grab the focus every few
  seconds (keystrokes in the first 1.5 seconds after that are discarded).
  Optionally the title bar or taskbar icon flashes, and the window shakes
  regularly while you are working elsewhere (Settings > Reminders).
  There you can also allow the question to be minimized; it then flashes in the
  taskbar until you open it again.
  It cannot be closed (the X is greyed out, Esc and Alt+F4 do nothing) –
  it only goes away with an entry.

Menu (right-click the clock face)
---------------------------------
Log now                          make an entry right away (also: double-click)
Edit entries...                  filter, edit (Ctrl+E), insert before (Ctrl+A),
                                 delete (Del), rename filtered entries
Import entries...                merge entries from another computer in time order
Sub-activities from text file... line 1 = activity (the beginning is enough),
                                 every further line = one sub-activity
Report...                        day, week, month, quarter, year or custom period,
                                 by activities, sub-activities or comments,
                                 daily details for copying to Excel
Settings...                      data folder, interval, autostart, hotkeys,
                                 hide activities from the report,
                                 reminders (minimize, flash, shake)

Files in the data folder
------------------------
taetigkeiten.kahraman    activities, sub-activities and templates
username.kahraman        your entries, one line per entry
Several people can share a folder on the network: each person has their own
entries file, the activity list is shared. Before every overwrite, Kahraman
creates a backup *.bak.
