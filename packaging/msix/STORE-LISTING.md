## English (en-US)

**Short description**
The time tracker that asks: a tiny tray app that regularly asks what you're working on and turns your answers into clear reports.

**Description**
Most time trackers wait for you to press start and stop – and you forget both. Kahraman comes to you: every few minutes (you choose how often) a small window asks what you are working on. Pick an activity, optionally a sub-activity and a comment, press Enter – done in two seconds. At the end of the week you know exactly how many hours went into which project.

Your data stays on your PC in plain text files in a folder you choose. No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Several people can share one data folder on a network drive. Kahraman is open source (Apache License 2.0).

**Features**
- Asks at your pace: every 1 minute to 24 hours, or right now via double-click or global hotkey
- Two-second entries with autocomplete; recently used activities on top
- Colour-coded activities and sub-activities, fold-away comment field
- Reports by day, week, month, quarter, year or any period – copy to Excel with one click
- Edit, filter, insert and rename past entries
- Hard to ignore: stays on top, optional flashing or a gentle shake
- Light and dark mode with a modern Windows 11 look
- 8 languages: English, German, Turkish, French, Italian, Arabic, Chinese, Russian
- Private by design: no account, no cloud, no telemetry

**Keywords** time tracking, timesheet, time tracker, working hours, project time, productivity, tray

**What's new in this version**
First version in the Microsoft Store.

会主动询问的时间记录工具：常驻任务栏的小应用，定时询问你在做什么，并把回答整理成清晰的报表。

数据保存在你自己的电脑上，以纯文本文件存放在你选择的文件夹中。无需账号，没有云端，没有遥测。多人可以共用网络驱动器上的同一个数据文件夹。Kahraman 是开源软件（Apache 2.0 许可证）。

**功能**
- 按你的节奏询问：1 分钟到 24 小时，也可双击或用全局快捷键立即记录
- 自动补全，两秒完成一条记录；最近用过的活动排在最前
- 活动和子活动可按颜色标记，备注栏可折叠
- 按日、周、月、季度、年或任意时间段生成报表——一键复制到 Excel
- 编辑、筛选、插入和重命名历史记录
- 不容忽视：始终置顶，可选闪烁或轻微抖动提醒
- 浅色和深色模式，现代 Windows 11 风格
- 8 种语言：中文、英语、德语、土耳其语、法语、意大利语、阿拉伯语、俄语
- 注重隐私：无需账号，没有云端，没有遥测

Большинство программ учёта времени ждут, что вы нажмёте «старт» и «стоп» — а про обе кнопки легко забыть. - Редактирование, фильтрация, вставка и переименование прошлых записей

- Светлая и тёмная тема в современном стиле Windows 11
